TapCare helps families support daily medication, vitamin, and supplement routines with simple check-ins, reminders, refill estimates, and optional family visibility.

Care for them without hovering.

TapCare is built for the in-between stage: someone is still independent, but a little more clarity would make daily routines feel calmer. The app keeps the routine simple for the person doing it and gives trusted family members a clearer way to support from a distance.

HOW IT WORKS
Set up a medication, vitamin, supplement, or daily routine.
Add reminder times and refill details if helpful.
Confirm a routine manually with one tap.
Pair an NFC tag to a bottle, tub, organizer, or tray if you want tap-to-confirm support.
Invite a trusted family member only if you want shared visibility.

FOR FAMILIES
TapCare helps reduce the everyday "Did you take it?" guessing.
Shared access is opt-in and revocable.
Family members can see simple routine status without changing the schedule.

FEATURES
Manual routine check-ins
Optional NFC tag check-ins
Custom schedules with multiple time slots
Local notifications and Live Activities for reminders
Refill estimates for day-to-day planning
Check-in history with source tracking
Optional family visibility
Data export for sharing or backup
Sign in with Apple or email for secure sync
Local use on one iPhone without creating an account

PRIVACY & SECURITY
Your health data is used for app functionality.
TapCare does not sell your data or use it for advertising.
No third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.
Privacy, support, consumer health data, and data deletion information are available in the app and on the web.

IMPORTANT: TapCare is a personal organization and routine-support tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always follow your healthcare provider's instructions.

NFC is optional. Manual check-ins work without NFC tags.
