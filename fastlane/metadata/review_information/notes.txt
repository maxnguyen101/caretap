TapCare is a routine support app for medication, vitamin, and supplement check-ins. Optional NFC tags make check-ins faster, but manual logging is always available. TapCare Premium and in-app purchases are not included in this release.

Reviewer-friendly test path:
1. Launch the app.
2. Choose "For me".
3. Choose "Continue on This iPhone" to use TapCare locally without creating an account.
4. Add a medication and schedule through the setup flow.
5. Skip NFC pairing if no NFC tag is available.
6. Manually log a check-in to test the full routine workflow without NFC hardware.
7. If NFC hardware is available, pair an NFC tag from setup or settings and tap the tag to log a dose.
8. Review settings for privacy, legal links, data export, and deletion/account options.

NFC is optional. Manual check-ins are available without NFC tags. A demo account is not required because the app supports local use on one iPhone. Optional sync sign-in is available with Sign in with Apple or email.

TapCare collects consumer health data such as medication names, vitamin or supplement names, schedules, reminder preferences, refill estimates, and check-in history solely for app functionality. No data is shared with third parties for advertising, and the app does not use third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

TapCare is a personal organization and routine-support tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Users should always follow their healthcare provider's instructions.
